## Images

Our logos and wordmarks are some of the most visually important parts of our brand identity. We treat these images with respect and care, and we ask that you do the same. Please follow these guidelines and instructions when using our assets.
Do not position or manipulate assets on this page in any way that may suggest endorsemnt or partnership without explicit permission.

> [!TIP]
>
> Hover over images on this page to download vector versions or change the color scheme.

### Rela

The Rela organisation is the parent of all our projects. These marks and images follow as the basis for other projects.

The word "Rela" should always be stylised in title case, or uppercase if included in an all-uppercase design.

#### Composite Logomark

This combination of our wordmark and logo should be used most often, where there is appropriate room to use it. If refering to the organisation and not the product, this logomark is a good way to distinguish them.

### RelaGit

RelaGit is the core product. These logos should be used instead of the above when specifically referring to RelaGit. The Rela mark should still be used for smaller spaces, although try to disambiguate from the organisation.

The word "RelaGit" should always have the "R" and "G" capitalised.

## Typography

Rela uses two different sets of typefaces in products and websites, although they are very similar in style.

> [!WARNING]
>
> The examples below may fall back to different fonts depending on your system and may not be accurate to the font.

### macOS

On macOS, or contexts where these fonts are available, they should be used over the alternatives.

#### SF Pro

SF Pro is used as our display and body font, it is produced by Apple and available as a [.dmg](https://developer.apple.com/design/resources/#fonts).

<div class="specimen sf-pro">&para; Sphinx of black quartz, judge my vow.</div>

#### SF Mono

SF Mono is our monospaced font. It is the default code font inside of RelaGit. It is also produced by Apple and available from the [same page as a .dmg](https://developer.apple.com/design/resources/#fonts).

<div class="specimen sf-mono">&para; Sphinx of black quartz, judge my vow.</div>

### Other contexts

These fonts are used when the macOS variants are not available

#### Inter

A body and display font with a similar design to SF Pro, available for all platforms from the [Inter website](https://rsms.me/inter/).

<div class="specimen inter">&para; Sphinx of black quartz, judge my vow.</div>

#### IBM Plex Mono

A monospaced font with similar letterforms to SF Mono, available for all platforms from the [IBM website](https://www.ibm.com/plex/).

<div class="specimen ibm-plex-mono">
    &para; Sphinx of black quartz, judge my vow.
</div>
